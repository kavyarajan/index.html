4 anchor blocks keep this system running even on broken days. They're marked in blue. Protect them above everything else.
Morning Engine

06:15
ANCHOR Wake up · don't negotiate
Alarm goes off. Sit up. Drink full glass of water immediately. Open curtains. No phone for first 10 minutes. This single habit changes the whole day.

06:45
MATCHA Matcha prep + meditation
This is your ignition ritual. Boil water (80°C, not boiling), sift matcha, whisk 30–40 sec until frothy. While it cools slightly — 10 min meditation. Sit, eyes closed, just follow breath. Headspace/Waking Up or just a timer. Matcha gives calm alertness (L-theanine + caffeine = no crash). Drink while sitting, not rushing.

ANCHOR 10-min daily plan

NPTEL Foundation Block · NPTEL / IIT lectures
First priority every morning until foundation is solid. Watch at 1.25–1.5x. Pause and redo derivations yourself on paper — don't just watch. When a concept clicks, move on. Use this window for ICDM + MDORS foundation first (your Aug 18 exam). 50 min on · 10 min off.

STUDY Lecture Slides Block · one subject
Go through lecture slides for the same topic you just built foundation on. Don't copy slides — translate into your own words. If NPTEL covered it: slides will feel fast. If stuck on a slide: go back to first principles in your notes, 5 min max, then move on. 50 min on · 10 min off.
